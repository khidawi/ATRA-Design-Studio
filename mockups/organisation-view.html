Questions to settle before building

    
  1. One organisation per ASTRA install, or several (for a consultancy managing client companies)?
  2. Are people always named individuals, or often just roles ("Support lead")? The mockup allows both.
  3. Should the HR export (CSV) and directory sync (Entra ID / Okta / LDAP) come later, so IDs match the company's real employee IDs?
  4. Who may edit the organisation: administrators only, or compliance as well?
  5. Is "reports to" enough for departments, or do you need teams inside departments?
